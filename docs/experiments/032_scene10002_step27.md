# Attempt 032: step-27 correction conditional on the fixed step-23 correction

## Question and predeclared contrast

Keep the final attempt-030 TD policy frozen. In scene 10002, keep step 23 fixed
to `north_large` (action 4) in both arms. Change only step 27 from the frozen
policy's selection to `north_small` (action 0) in the treatment arm. Test whether
this additional action correction restores complete-episode success.

The control is the corrected trajectory from attempt 031, not the original
unassisted policy. Its first reference disagreement is step 27, after 26 matching
reference actions with the step-23 intervention included. At the identical input,
Q(east_large) = 87.21077728271484 and Q(north_small) = 87.17753601074219;
the greedy-minus-reference gap is 0.03324127197265625.

## Execution and verification budget

1. Reload the full frozen snapshot and reproduce the attempt-031 corrected
   control exactly: all 200 transitions, Q vectors, final metrics and reset data.
2. Reload again and execute the treatment with exactly the two specified overrides.
   Require an identical complete prefix through step 26, including the common
   step-23 correction, and identical encoded input, position and Q vector at
   step 27. Every other action must be the current frozen policy's argmax.
3. Independently reload both arms through the normal `training.evaluate` loop.
   Verify every transition, final metric, reset geometry and certification record
   against the primary manual loop. These are replication checks, not new models.

Four complete episodes are predeclared: two primary and two verification
episodes. Count executed actions, overrides and reset-certification simulation
actions separately. The full scripted reference and unassisted failure were
already verified in attempt 031 and are not rerun here.

Record full Q vectors, encoded input/successor hashes, positions, actions,
rewards, end flags and environment info. Record any later reference disagreement
without intervening. Preserve failed attempts and exceptions. Hash sources and
inputs before/after the run; archive raw bytes without Git newline conversion.
Refuse to overwrite an existing run. Commit/push this protocol and implementation
before new simulator work, then commit/push the complete results.

A successful result would show that the extra step-27 correction is sufficient
conditional on the fixed step-23 correction for this frozen known-scene policy.
It would not establish that step 27 alone rescues the unassisted policy, that
the policy learned either action, or that unseen-scene performance improved.
